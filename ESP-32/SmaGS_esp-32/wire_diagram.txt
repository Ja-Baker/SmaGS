ESP32 SmaGS wire diagram
POWER CONNECTIONS

ESP32 3.3V Pin  ->  Connect to VCC of DHT11, SHT31, and SM100

ESP32 GND Pin   ->  Connect to GND of all sensors and all LED cathodes (short legs)

SENSOR DATA PINS

GPIO 4          ->  DHT11 Data Pin

GPIO 21 (SDA)   ->  SHT31 Yellow Wire

GPIO 22 (SCL)   ->  SHT31 Green Wire

GPIO 34 (Analog)->  SM100 Signal/Data Pin

LED PINS (Use 220 ohm resistors on each)

GPIO 12         ->  Red LED (Error)

GPIO 13         ->  Green LED (WiFi)

GPIO 14         ->  Blue LED (Pump)

SHT31 WIRE COLOR KEY (FS304)

Brown:  3.3V

Black:  GND

Yellow: SDA (GPIO 21)

Green:  SCL (GPIO 22)
